Florals & Pollinators

Intimate observations of flowers in bloom and the essential work of pollinators. These paintings celebrate the delicate, life-giving relationship between blossom and bee — moments of color, light, and quiet interdependence.

Studio moment captured at The Roger Tory Peterson Institute while working on the Florals & Pollinators series. “The Last Pollen” continues to evolve — that quiet final act of the bloom.
